## About

University of Toronto and the Vector Institute. Lead author of the paper that introduced
proof-of-learning, and a co-author of the paper that broke it. The pair together is the clearest
statement in this wiki of the gap between a compute inequality asserted as a desideratum and a compute
inequality proved.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Proof-of-Learning: Definitions and Practice](../sources/proof-of-learning.md)**
  (Jia, Yaghini, Choquette-Choo, Dullerud, Thudi, Chandrasekaran, Papernot; 42nd IEEE Symposium on
  Security and Privacy, 2021) — joint lead author. Introduced `E[C_V] ≤ E[C_T]`, `E[C_T] ≤ E[C_A]`, and
  the dishonest-spoof threshold `E[C_A] < E[C_T]`, all as requirements rather than theorems, plus
  Conjecture 1 in place of the missing hardness proof.
- **[Proof-of-Learning is Currently More Broken Than You Think](../sources/pol-more-broken.md)**
  (Fang, Jia, Thudi, Yaghini, Choquette-Choo, Dullerud, Chandrasekaran, Papernot; arXiv:2208.03567v2,
  2023) — joint lead author. Two reproducible spoofing attacks, and the formalisation of the
  reproducibility, representativeness, and cheapness assumptions.
- **[On the Necessity of Auditable Algorithmic Definitions for Machine Unlearning](../sources/auditable-unlearning-definitions.md)**
  (Thudi, Jia, Shumailov, Papernot; USENIX Security 2022) — co-author.
