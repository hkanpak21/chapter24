## About

Courant Institute, New York University (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDF). Works on high-dimensional probability, random landscapes, spin glasses, and the dynamics of stochastic optimization.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Online Stochastic Gradient Descent on Non-Convex Losses from High-Dimensional Inference](../sources/information-exponent-online-sgd.md)** (Ben Arous, Gheissari, Jagannath; 2021) — introduces the [information exponent](../concepts/information-exponent.md) and proves matching online-SGD sample-complexity thresholds.
- Cited in this corpus (Ben Arous, Gerbelot, Piccolo) for SGD over the Stiefel manifold in the multi-spiked tensor PCA model.
