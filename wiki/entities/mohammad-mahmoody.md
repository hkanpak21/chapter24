## About

Theoretical computer science researcher working at the intersection of cryptography, complexity theory, and adversarial machine learning. Primary PhD advisor/collaborator of Saeed Mahloujifar.

## Contributions / Works

- Co-author on the entire body of work covered in [A Complexity Theoretic Approach to Adversarial ML](../sources/complexity-theoretic-adversarial-ml.md)
- [DMM18]: Adversarial risk and robustness definitions (NeurIPS 2018)
- [MDM19]: Curse of concentration in robust learning (AAAI 2019)
- [MM17]: Blockwise p-tampering attacks (TCC 2017)
- [MM19]: Can Adversarially Robust Learning Leverage Computational Hardness? (ALT 2019)
- [EMM20]: Computational Concentration of Measure: Optimal Bounds, Reductions, and More (SODA 2020)
- **[Adversarially Robust Learning Could Leverage Computational Hardness](../sources/computational-hardness-robust-learning.md)** (ALT 2020, GJMM20)
- **[Overparameterization from Computational Constraints](../sources/overparameterization-computational-constraints.md)** (2022, GJMMW22) — computational efficiency of the learner priced in model parameters; bounding the attacker buys the parameters back
- [MMM19]: Universal Multi-Party Poisoning Attacks (ICML 2019)
- **[Fine-Grained NIKE: Constructions and Lower Bounds](../sources/fine-grained-nike-lower-bounds.md)** (2023) — with Afshar, Couteau, Sadeghi. Merkle generalized to `K` parties in the ROM; the `O(n^2)`-query break of 3-NIKE in Maurer's generic group model
