## About

IBM Research Almaden at the time of the work catalogued here. Works on generalization, adaptivity, and the
interaction between privacy and statistical validity.

## Contributions / Works

- [Preventing False Discovery in Interactive Data Analysis is Hard](../sources/preventing-false-discovery-hard.md)
  (HU14) — the `n^{3+o(1)}` adaptive-query hardness result under one-way functions, tight to within a factor
  `n` against the Dwork–Feldman–Hardt–Pitassi–Reingold–Roth upper bounds. The framing that "achieving
  statistical validity alone can be a source of computational intractability in adaptive settings" is his and
  Ullman's.
- Co-author of the DFHPRR line establishing the positive side — DP as a stability condition implying
  generalization — which supplies the upper bounds the lower bound is measured against.
