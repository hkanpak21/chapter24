## About

Associate Professor at Princeton CS. Works on sum-of-squares, robust statistics, and algorithmic statistical-computational tradeoffs.

## Contributions / Works

- **[A Nearly Tight Sum-of-Squares Lower Bound for the Planted Clique Problem](../sources/barak-sos-lower-bound-planted-clique.md)** (Barak, Hopkins, Kelner, Kothari, Moitra, Potechin; FOCS 2016 / SICOMP 2019) — co-developer of pseudo-calibration.
- **[The Quasi-Polynomial Low-Degree Conjecture is False](../sources/quasi-poly-low-degree-conjecture-false.md)**
  (Buhai, Hsieh, Jain, Kothari; 2025) — disproves Hopkins' low-degree conjecture in the regime it was
  stated, separating "low-degree-bounded adversary" from "quasi-polynomial-time adversary".
- **[Rigorous Implications of the Low-Degree Heuristic](../sources/rigorous-implications-low-degree.md)**
  (Hsieh, Kane, Kothari, Li, Mohanty, Tiegel; 2026) — the positive counterpart, establishing what
  vanishing low-degree advantage does rule out, under permutation invariance and noise.
- Line of SOS-based algorithms for robust statistics.
