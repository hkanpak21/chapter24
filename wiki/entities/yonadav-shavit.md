## About

Harvard University (per the affiliation in the ingested PDF). Works on the technical feasibility of
governing large-scale ML development, and specifically on what would have to be true of the underlying
verification primitives before a compute-based regime could be enforced.

## Contributions / Works

- **[What does it take to catch a Chinchilla? Verifying Rules on Large-Scale Neural Network Training via
  Compute Monitoring](../sources/catch-a-chinchilla-compute-monitoring.md)** (arXiv:2303.11341v2, 2023) —
  a three-layer monitoring framework (on-chip weight snapshots, transcript reporting, supply-chain
  accounting), a sampling bound for inspectors, Theorem 1 on chip-splitting evasion, and Definition 1 of
  **Proof-of-Training-Transcript**, parameterised as `J`-efficient and `F`-hard. The `F`-hardness half is
  defined and explicitly left open.
