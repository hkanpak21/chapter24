## About

Information, Learning and Physics Laboratory, EPFL (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDFs). Works on message passing, high-dimensional inference, and computational phase transitions in learning.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Benefits of Reusing Batches for Gradient Descent in Two-Layer Networks](../sources/reusing-batches-breaks-exponents.md)** (Dandi, Troiani, Arnaboldi, Pesce, Zdeborová, Krzakala; 2024) — the batch-reuse escape from the information and leap exponents, via rigorous DMFT.
- **[A Noise Sensitivity Exponent Controls Large Statistical-to-Computational Gaps](../sources/noise-sensitivity-exponent.md)** (Defilippis, Krzakala, Loureiro, Maillard; 2026) — the [noise sensitivity exponent](../concepts/noise-sensitivity-exponent.md) for AMP in the proportional regime, plus a sustained brittleness critique of the information, leap and generative exponents.
