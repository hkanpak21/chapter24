## About

CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security. Works on privacy attacks against machine learning —
membership inference, model inversion, and data reconstruction — and on systematizing them.
Corresponding author on the reconstruction SoK in this wiki.

*Disambiguation:* distinct from [Xiao Zhang](xiao-zhang.md), and from the Binchi Zhang of
[Verification of Machine Unlearning is Fragile](../sources/unlearning-verification-fragile.md).
