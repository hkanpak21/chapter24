## About

Cryptography researcher; affiliated with UC Berkeley at the time of GJMMW22 (the only affiliation
stated in the source read here). Contributes the coding-theoretic and randomness-extraction machinery
— Reed–Solomon list decoding, small-bias masking, Vadhan's sampler — that carries the parameter lower
bounds in the work below.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Overparameterization from Computational Constraints](../sources/overparameterization-computational-constraints.md)**
  (2022, GJMMW22) — with Garg, Jha, Mahloujifar, Mahmoody. Model parameters as the resource a learner
  is priced in, separated by whether the learner is computationally bounded.
