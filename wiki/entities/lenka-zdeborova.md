## About

Statistical Physics Of Computation Laboratory, EPFL (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDF). Works on statistical-physics methods for inference and learning, including message passing, replica computations, and computational phase transitions.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Benefits of Reusing Batches for Gradient Descent in Two-Layer Networks](../sources/reusing-batches-breaks-exponents.md)** (Dandi, Troiani, Arnaboldi, Pesce, Zdeborová, Krzakala; 2024) — proves that multi-pass GD with batch reuse escapes the information- and leap-exponent barriers, disproving the Abbe–Boix-Adserà–Misiakiewicz conjecture and reducing sample complexity to `O(d)` for a broad class.
- Cited throughout this corpus (Barbier–Krzakala–Macris–Miolane–Zdeborová) for the information-theoretic thresholds of generalized linear models that the AMP-versus-IT gap statements are built on.
