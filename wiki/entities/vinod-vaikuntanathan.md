## About

Professor at MIT (CSAIL). Leading researcher in lattice-based cryptography, fully homomorphic encryption, and connections between cryptography and learning theory.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Planting Undetectable Backdoors in ML Models](../sources/planting-undetectable-backdoors.md)** (FOCS 2022) — with Goldwasser, Kim, Zamir
- **[Win-Win Robust Learning](../sources/win-win-robust-learning.md)** (COLT 2019) — with Degwekar, Nakkiran
- **[Fine-grained Cryptography](../sources/fine-grained-cryptography.md)** (2016) — with Degwekar, Vasudevan. The paper that names fine-grained cryptography and gives it its class-based formulation: unconditional primitives against `AC0`, and PKE/CRHF against `NC1` from `⊕L/poly ⊄ NC1`
- Key work on fully homomorphic encryption (BGV scheme), lattice-based PRFs, LWE-based cryptography
