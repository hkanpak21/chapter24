## About

Professor at Bocconi University. Co-author of the Ball–Rosen–Sabin–Vasudevan line that started
fine-grained cryptography from fine-grained complexity assumptions, and of the survey
"Fine-grained cryptography: A new frontier?" that the field cites for its framing.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Planted Orthogonal Vectors Problem](../sources/planted-orthogonal-vectors.md)** (2025, with Kühnemann, Polak) — a planted `k`-OV distribution with `(k−1)`-wise independent marginals, its uniqueness, two search-to-decision reductions, and the conjecture that it is `n^{k−o(1)}`-hard on average.
- The Ball–Rosen–Sabin–Vasudevan average-case fine-grained hardness and proof-of-work papers are cited throughout this corpus as the origin of the worst-case-OV-to-cryptography route; they are not themselves ingested here.
