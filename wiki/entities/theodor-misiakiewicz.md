## About

Department of Statistics, Stanford University (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDFs). Works on high-dimensional asymptotics of kernel and neural-network learning.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Merged-Staircase Property](../sources/merged-staircase-property.md)** (Abbe, Boix-Adserà, Misiakiewicz; 2022).
- **[SGD Learning on Neural Networks: Leap Complexity and Saddle-to-Saddle Dynamics](../sources/leap-complexity-sgd.md)** (Abbe, Boix-Adserà, Misiakiewicz; 2023).
- Cited in this corpus as a co-author (with Joshi and Srebro) of the SQ-exponent characterisation for sparse juntas, which [The Generative Leap](../sources/generative-leap-multi-index.md) and [DIKR25](../sources/robust-multi-index-sq-bounds.md) both invoke for the SQ side of the multi-index picture.
