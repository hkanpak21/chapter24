## About

Founding director of the CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security. In this wiki he appears on
the systematization of privacy attacks against machine learning models.

## Contributions / Works

- **[SoK: Data Reconstruction Attacks Against Machine Learning Models: Definition, Metrics, and Benchmark](../sources/sok-data-reconstruction-attacks.md)**
  (Wen, Liu, Backes, Zhang; 2025) — the statement that data reconstruction has no consensus formal
  definition or metrics, plus a proposed taxonomy, `(ε, µ)`-approximate reconstruction, and a
  ten-attack benchmark.
