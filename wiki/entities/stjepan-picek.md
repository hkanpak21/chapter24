## About

Researcher at Radboud University (previously Delft University of Technology), working on machine learning security with an emphasis on backdoor attacks and defenses, and on systematic evaluation methodology. Senior author on the first systematization of knowledge devoted to backdoor *defense evaluation* rather than to attacks or defenses themselves.

## Contributions / Works

- **[SoK: The Last Line of Defense — On Backdoor Defense Evaluation (AKK+25)](../sources/sok-backdoor-defense-evaluation.md)** (venue unconfirmed from PDF; arXiv 2025, last author) — meta-analysis of 183 backdoor defense papers from nine A* venues plus 3 000+ re-runs over 170 GPU-days; finds published defense claims contradicted on harder datasets and stealthier attacks, and makes threat-model assumptions a first-class extraction field.

## Connections

- Co-authors on AKK+25 include Gorka Abad, Marina Krček, Stefanos Koffas, Behrad Tajalli, Marco Arazzi, Roberto Riaño, Xiaoyun Xu, Zhuoran Liu and Antonino Nocera.
- Same critique-of-practice register as [Konrad Rieck](konrad-rieck.md) and [Lorenzo Cavallaro](lorenzo-cavallaro.md) on [Dos and Don'ts of ML in Computer Security](../sources/dos-and-donts-ml-security.md), which AKK+25's threat-model findings instantiate at the backdoor level.
