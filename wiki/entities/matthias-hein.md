## About

Professor at the University of Tübingen. Co-author, with Francesco Croce, of AutoAttack and of AutoAttack's two companion standardization works. Research spans certified and empirical robustness; his group's DFG funding (grant 389792660, TRR 248) supports the test-time-defence evaluation.

## Contributions / Works

- **[RobustBench: a standardized adversarial robustness benchmark (CAS+21)](../sources/robustbench.md)** (NeurIPS 2021, Datasets and Benchmarks track).
- **[Evaluating the Adversarial Robustness of Adaptive Test-time Defenses (CGB+22)](../sources/adaptive-test-time-defenses-eval.md)** (ICML 2022).
- AutoAttack and APGD (Croce & Hein 2020), cited throughout the batch; not itself ingested.
