## About

Mathematics Institute, EPFL (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDFs). Works on information theory, community detection, and the complexity of SGD learning on neural networks.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Merged-Staircase Property](../sources/merged-staircase-property.md)** (Abbe, Boix-Adserà, Misiakiewicz; 2022) — MSP as a necessary and nearly sufficient condition for `O(d)`-sample SGD learning of sparse functions on depth-2 mean-field networks over the hypercube.
- **[SGD Learning on Neural Networks: Leap Complexity and Saddle-to-Saddle Dynamics](../sources/leap-complexity-sgd.md)** (Abbe, Boix-Adserà, Misiakiewicz; 2023) — the [leap complexity](../concepts/leap-complexity.md), the `Theta~(d^{max(Leap,2)})` conjecture, and CSQ lower bounds.
