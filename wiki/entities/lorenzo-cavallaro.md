## About

Professor at University College London at the time of the work catalogued here (previously King's College London), working on machine learning for security with an emphasis on Android malware detection, concept drift, and the realism of experimental evaluation in that setting.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Dos and Don'ts of Machine Learning in Computer Security (AQP+22)](../sources/dos-and-donts-ml-security.md)** (USENIX Security 2022) — co-author on the ten-pitfall taxonomy; the paper's mobile malware detection impact analysis (pitfalls P1, P4, P7) sits in his area.
