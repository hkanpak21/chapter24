## About

PhD student at PACM, Princeton University (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDFs). Works on the sample complexity of feature learning and on the computational-statistical interface for index models.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Computational-Statistical Gaps in Gaussian Single-Index Models](../sources/generative-exponent-single-index.md)** (Damian, Pillaud-Vivien, Lee, Bruna; 2024) — introduces the [generative exponent](../concepts/generative-exponent.md), with matching SQ / low-degree lower bounds and a partial-trace upper bound at `d^{k*/2}`.
- **[The Generative Leap](../sources/generative-leap-multi-index.md)** (Damian, Lee, Bruna; 2025) — the multi-index extension, with a low-degree lower bound and a Hermite-kernel U-statistic upper bound at `d^{1 ∨ k*/2}`.
