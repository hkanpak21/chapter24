## About

Professor at MIT CSAIL. A founder of fine-grained complexity, and the bridge between it and
cryptography: co-author both of the paper that builds public-key cryptography from a fine-grained
average-case hypothesis and of the paper that supplies average-case hardness for the field's core
problems.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Public-Key Cryptography in the Fine-Grained Setting](../sources/pkc-fine-grained-setting.md)** (2019, with LaVigne, Lincoln) — Definition 5, which names "the gap"; the plantable/list-hard/splittable property checklist; fine-grained key exchange and PKC from the Zero-`k`-Clique hypothesis, with gap `N` to `N^{2−4/(k+2)}`.
- **[Average-Case Hardness of Parity Problems: Orthogonal Vectors, k-SUM and More](../sources/average-case-parity-problems.md)** (2025, with Dalirrooyfard, Lincoln, Saha) — the first average-case super-linear lower bounds for parity variants of `k`-OV, `k`-SUM, `k`-XOR, on explicit distributions.
