## About

Yale University (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDF). Works on the computational complexity of statistical estimation and learning.

## Contributions / Works

- **[On the Hardness of Learning One Hidden Layer Neural Networks](../sources/hardness-one-hidden-layer.md)** (Li, Zadik, Zampetakis; 2024) — cryptographic hardness of weakly learning width-`omega(sqrt(d log d))` one-hidden-layer ReLU networks under Gaussian inputs and polynomially small *Gaussian* noise, via [Continuous LWE](../concepts/continuous-lwe.md) and GapSVP.
