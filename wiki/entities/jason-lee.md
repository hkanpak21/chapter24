## About

Professor in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, Princeton University (affiliation as stated in the ingested PDFs). Works on optimization and sample complexity for feature learning in neural networks.

**Not to be confused with** [Yin Tat Lee](yin-tat-lee.md), a different author already in this wiki.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Computational-Statistical Gaps in Gaussian Single-Index Models](../sources/generative-exponent-single-index.md)** (Damian, Pillaud-Vivien, Lee, Bruna; 2024) — the [generative exponent](../concepts/generative-exponent.md).
- **[The Generative Leap](../sources/generative-leap-multi-index.md)** (Damian, Lee, Bruna; 2025) — the [generative leap exponent](../concepts/generative-leap-exponent.md).
- Cited throughout this corpus as a co-author of the CSQ lower bound for single-index models (Damian–Lee–Soltanolkotabi) and of the loss-smoothing algorithm that attains it (Damian–Nichani–Ge–Lee).
