## About

Researcher at the University of Pennsylvania (previously Johns Hopkins University), working on subspace clustering, sparse representation and generalized principal component analysis, and on applying that geometric machinery to adversarial robustness.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Adversarial Examples Might be Avoidable — The Role of Data Concentration in Adversarial Robustness (PSV23)](../sources/data-concentration-robustness-avoidable.md)** (NeurIPS 2023, last author) — shows that concentration of data mass on small-*volume* subsets is necessary for a robust classifier to exist (Brunn–Minkowski), and that a strengthened version is sufficient; then builds polyhedral, norm-independent certificates for data near a union of low-dimensional linear subspaces. The union-of-subspaces machinery is his own line of work turned toward robustness.
