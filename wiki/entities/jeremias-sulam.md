## About

Researcher at Johns Hopkins University, working on sparse representation, dictionary learning and the theory of structured signal models, with recent work applying those tools to certified adversarial robustness.

## Contributions / Works

- **[Adversarial Examples Might be Avoidable — The Role of Data Concentration in Adversarial Robustness (PSV23)](../sources/data-concentration-robustness-avoidable.md)** (NeurIPS 2023) — co-author. The certified defense is built from the dual of an ℓ1 sparse-representation problem, yielding polyhedral certified regions described by selected training points rather than ℓp balls, and certifying perturbations randomized smoothing cannot.
