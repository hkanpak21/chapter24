## About

Adobe Research (per the affiliation in the ingested PDF). Works on algorithms and lower bounds for the
linear-algebraic primitives inside deep learning — attention, gradients, sketching — often with
fine-grained complexity as the lower-bound tool.

## Contributions / Works

- **[The Fine-Grained Complexity of Gradient Computation for Training Large Language Models](../sources/fine-grained-gradient-llm.md)**
  (Alman, Song; arXiv:2402.04497v1, 2024) — matching `n^{1+o(1)}` upper bound and SETH-conditional
  `n^{2−o(1)}` lower bound for approximate attention-loss gradient computation, separated by a threshold
  in the entry-magnitude parameter.
