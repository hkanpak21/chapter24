Formal results on the security of machine learning are hard to compare, because a
claim about an attack rarely states what the attacker may spend. We read
\NumSources{} formal works and record for each one which party's resources enter
its main claim and in what unit. We then give an experiment with four coordinates:
where the adversary observes on the path from training data to model output, what
it must produce and to what tolerance, a budget held per party, and what public
knowledge alone already achieves. The mapping leaves \NumUnstated{} of
\NumSources{} budgets unstated and four regions of the experiment empty.
