# Contract for 01-introduction

## The claim of this section in one sentence

The field describes its adversaries and does not price them, and cryptography already holds the four objects a comparison needs.

## Topic sentences, one per paragraph, in order

1. Security claims about machine learning appear faster than the field can compare them.
2. Membership inference makes the defect concrete, and we use it to state three problems.
3. Membership inference is one attack among many, so no single attack runs through every part of the survey.
4. The standing systematization sorts attacks along three parts, none of them a computational resource.
5. Cryptography holds the objects that comparison needs: an adversary class, a budget, a success criterion with a tolerance, and a baseline.
6. We record the budget per party and not per adversary, which makes the most common answer visible.
