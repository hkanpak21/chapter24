# Contract for 00-abstract

## The claim of this section in one sentence

Formal claims about the security of machine learning cannot be compared, because
almost none of them states what the attacker may spend.

## Topic sentences, one per paragraph, in order

1. Security claims about machine learning are hard to compare, because a claim about an attack rarely states what the attacker may spend.
2. We read 200 formal works and record, for each one, which party's resources enter its main claim, in what unit, and how tightly.
3. We quote the deciding sentence from each work and check it against the source document.
4. Thirty-seven of the works bound no party at all.
5. Works studying a named attack bound nobody more often, in direction and not in size.
6. Only 18 works state a bound as a relation between two parties' work.
7. Where the attack literature does bound an adversary, it bounds what the adversary may change.
8. We identify 19 works holding a resource quantity their own framing does not carry.

## What this section must not do

Carry a priority claim. The venue's SoK award paper makes its priority claim in
the contribution list and not in the abstract. Carry emphasis of any kind. Claim
a size the interval does not support.
