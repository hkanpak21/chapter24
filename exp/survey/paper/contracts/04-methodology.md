# Contract for 04-methodology

## The claim of this section in one sentence

A fixed vocabulary, four decision rules and a deciding quotation per row make the classification something a reader can dispute cell by cell.

## Topic sentences, one per paragraph, in order

1. The corpus holds 206 documents, of which 200 carry a formal claim and enter the classification.
2. We classify each work on thirteen fields against a fixed vocabulary, so that two readers assign the same value.
3. Four rules decide the cases where a work admits more than one reading.
4. We checked every quotation against the primary document, in two passes, and the second caught what the first passed.
5. Three limits bear on the counts, and we state each one.
