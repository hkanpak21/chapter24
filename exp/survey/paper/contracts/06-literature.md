# Contract for 06-literature

## The claim of this section in one sentence

Ordering the corpus by constrained party shows what each region contains and the boundary its characteristic result imposes.

## Topic sentences, one per paragraph, in order

1. The largest region bounds an adversary in running time or circuit size.
2. The next region bounds what the adversary may alter and leaves what it may spend open.
3. Sixty-five works constrain the party that trains.
4. Thirty-seven works constrain no party at all, in two distinct lines.
5. Ten works bound an auditor and seven a mechanism.
6. Fine-grained cryptography names the two-party relation and contains no machine learning.
7. The scaling literature measures training compute precisely and the security literature never states an adversary's budget in it.

## What this section must not do

Illustrate a region only with works the classification places in that region.
