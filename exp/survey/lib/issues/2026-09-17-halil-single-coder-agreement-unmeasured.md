# One reader coded every row, and the paper says so

## What this is

Each of the 200 works was classified once. The only agreement figure on record
is 24 of 27 rows where two readers overlapped, which is an overlap and not a
measurement. Section 4 states the limit, and the fixed vocabulary and the four
decision rules stand in for the missing statistic.

A reviewer who treats the classification as the paper's evidence will ask for an
agreement statistic, because the classification is the paper. A second
independent pass over a sample of about 40 rows with a reported agreement
statistic would answer it, and it costs one session.

Whether that is worth doing before submission is a judgment about reviewer
tolerance, and the venue profile records no house expectation either way.

## What closes it

A second independent coding pass over a named sample with its agreement
statistic reported in Section 4, or a ruling that the stated limit is enough.
