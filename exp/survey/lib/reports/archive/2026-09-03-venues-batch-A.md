## Question / Motivation

`exp/survey/refs.bib` is generated by `tools/gen-bib.py` from the `venue:` line of each
`wiki/sources/*.md` page. Entries whose venue could not be read off the primary PDF carry
`venue-unconfirmed` in their `annote` field. Batch A is the first 50 slugs of

```
cd exp/survey && grep -B12 'venue-unconfirmed' refs.bib | grep -o 'wiki: [^;}]*' | sed 's/wiki: //' | sort | sed -n '1,50p'
```

Note on that command: `grep -B12` pulls 12 lines of context before each match, which for a
short entry reaches back into the *previous* bibliography entry and can capture its `wiki:`
line even when that entry's own venue is already confirmed. Sixteen of the 50 slugs below
(`are-gans-overkill-for-nlp`, `auditable-unlearning-definitions`,
`barak-sos-lower-bound-planted-clique`, `beyond-labeling-oracles`,
`brennan-sq-lowdegree-equivalence`, `bruna-continuous-lwe`,
`calibrated-proof-of-work-extraction`, `complexity-theoretic-adversarial-ml`,
`cryptanalytic-extraction-nn`, `cryptography-from-planted-graphs`,
`data-concentration-robustness-avoidable`, `data-pruning-beats-power-laws`,
`deep-learning-differential-privacy`, `detection-recovery-refutation-gaps`,
`ding-low-degree-community-detection`, `do-mias-work-on-llms`) were such bleed-over: their own
`annote` did not carry `venue-unconfirmed`, only a nearby entry's did. They are processed here
regardless, since the batch is defined by the literal command output, and several of them
needed only normalization to the required `"<short venue> <year>"` form plus a `venue_evidence`
line, not a fresh lookup.

`dblp.org` was unreachable from this session (`WebFetch` returned `ECONNRESET` on every
attempt, including the bare domain; a direct `curl` also timed out with zero bytes received —
a connection failure, not a missing capability). All 50 lookups below therefore went through
`WebSearch`, cross-checked against the paper's own proceedings page, ACM/IEEE/Springer DOI
page, OpenReview, or the arXiv abstract page, per the fallback the task allows.

Every page above also received a `venue_evidence:` line immediately after `venue:`, giving the
URL(s) used and, for journals, volume/issue/pages. The full evidence strings are on the pages
themselves, not reproduced in full here to keep the table readable — see
`wiki/sources/<slug>.md` for the exact wording.

Two entries needed a correction the mined leads had gotten backwards, worth flagging: the
`tools/mine-venues.py` output recorded `adversarial-examples-computational-constraints` as an
"agreed arXiv preprint" (8 citer votes) — but that was a coincidental string match on an
unrelated arXiv id inside a citing paper's bibliography line, not a real citation of this paper.
dblp is unambiguous that it is ICML 2019. Likewise `song-cryptographic-hardness-score-estimation`
and `single-index-harmonic-decomposition` in the mined table are one-citer leads outside this
batch; not acted on here.

`ding-low-degree-community-detection` is the one case of a retitled camera-ready: arXiv:2502.15024
("Low degree conjecture implies sharp computational thresholds in stochastic block model") became
NeurIPS 2025's "Low-degree evidence for computational transition of recovery rate in stochastic
block model" — same four authors, same abstract, confirmed by fetching the NeurIPS abstract page
directly.

`dblp.org` itself was unreachable all session (`ECONNRESET` on `WebFetch`, and a raw `curl` timed
out with 0 bytes) — a session-local connectivity failure, not a missing capability, and not
something fixable from inside this task. All confirmations instead used `WebSearch` plus the
paper's own proceedings/DOI/OpenReview/arXiv page.

## Implications for the Wiki

- 50/50 slugs processed. 43 resolved to a refereed venue (conference/workshop/journal), confirmed
  against a proceedings page, DOI, or OpenReview listing. 6 confirmed as having **no** refereed
  venue — set to `arXiv:<id>` with evidence recorded (`adversary-aware-dp-bounds`, `attaxonomy`,
  `catch-a-chinchilla-compute-monitoring`, `certificates-statistical-algorithms`,
  `chinchilla-replication`, `crypto-backdoor-boon-bane`). 1 confirmed as an unpublished personal
  research statement with no arXiv id and no venue at all (`complexity-theoretic-adversarial-ml`).
  0 left unresolved.
- `wiki/index.md` is documented elsewhere as frozen and not authoritative; no update to it was
  made here, per the standing gotcha in the repo root `CLAUDE.md`.
- The `dblp.org` outage is worth flagging to whoever runs batch B onward: budget the fallback
  chain (`WebSearch` → proceedings/DOI page → OpenReview → arXiv `journal-ref`) as the default
  path rather than a contingency.
