# PI meeting, 2026-08-05

## What was asked for

A survey, framed as **bridging the gap between theory and practice**, in three steps.

1. **Find what has been done in terms of taxonomy.** Survey the existing systematizations before
   proposing anything.
2. **Build the taxonomy as a matrix**: privacy and integrity, against train time and test time, against
   model, learning algorithm and dataset.
3. *"Farklı çalışmalarda ortak aspectler ile birlikte bir tanım."* — a definition, together with the
   aspects common across different works.

## What the survey must define

Named in the meeting as the objects the definition has to cover:

- learning
- model
- dataset
- train-time and test-time attacks
- adversaries
- and the existing definitions in the field, subsumed

## The contribution

**The definitions.** Stated explicitly as the contribution both to the field and to this programme. The
taxonomy and the survey of prior taxonomies are the setting for the definitions, not the deliverable.

## Decisions taken after the meeting

- Scope is treated as settled and not reopened.
- The survey is a **position paper carrying a definitional contribution**, not a neutral survey. Its
  structure follows from that: prior taxonomies are reviewed to establish that the definitional slot is
  open, not for completeness of coverage.
- The matrix is a *presentation*. It is generated by axes rather than enumerated, so that cells which
  are structurally empty can be shown to be empty rather than merely unlisted. See
  [[2026-08-05-halil-lit-survey-structure]].

## Open with the PI

- Whether availability belongs alongside privacy and integrity as a third goal, since the requested
  matrix has two.
- Whether the scope includes large language models and agentic systems, or classical supervised
  learning only. This changes the ingest list substantially.
- Target venue, which fixes length and whether formal definitions can carry proofs or only statements.
